Jane voice

Jane voice is a small personal tool, used by its owner only. It is not a public service and has no other users.

It lets the owner speak a request to their own smart speaker ("add a task: Jane, ..."). The tool reads that task from the owner's own Google Tasks, hands it to the owner's personal assistant running on their home server, and marks the task done.

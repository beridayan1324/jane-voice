Jane voice privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Jane voice is a personal tool used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

What it accesses

  • Google Tasks in the owner's account: it reads open tasks and marks the ones meant for the assistant as done.
  • Nothing else in the Google account: no email, contacts, files, calendar or location.

What it does with it

  • Tasks that start with "Jane" are passed to the owner's personal assistant on the owner's home server.
  • No data is sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.
  • The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's home server.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
